What is an ERP for colleges?

ERP stands for Enterprise Resource Planning. In a college, it replaces scattered registers and spreadsheets with one connected system, so every department works from the same up-to-date records.

  • Less paperwork. Admissions, fees and records are handled online.
  • Live information. Attendance, marks and timetables update as they change.
  • Right access for each role. Admins, faculty and students see only what they need.
  • Better decisions. Reports show trends in attendance, results and fee collection.
